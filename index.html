HuntKit Privacy Policy

Effective October 2, 2026

HuntKit does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

Your data stays on your device

Everything you enter in HuntKit, including gear, firearms, ammunition, loads, and setups, is stored only on your device. It never leaves your device unless you choose to export a backup file, and you control where that file goes.

No tracking

HuntKit does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind, and it does not require an account.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling HuntKit deletes all of its data from your device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, for example when optional in-app purchases are added, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email huntkitsupport@gmail.com.
